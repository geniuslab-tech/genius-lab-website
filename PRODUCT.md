## Product Purpose

Genius Lab helps companies connect fragmented systems and transform fragmented business data into actionable intelligence. It connects business systems, integrates and engineers their data, builds modern analytics and business intelligence, and uses advanced AI to understand the company's business context.

That intelligence layer becomes a "Second Brain" for the organization. On top of it, AI Agents understand the business end to end: systems, data tables, metrics, numbers, dashboards, processes, customers, operations, financial information and context. The goal is valuable insight, visibility, understanding and better executive decisions.

## Positioning

Genius Lab is two things at once:
1. A technology and data services company (expertise across data engineering, analytics, BI and AI).
2. A company building its own proprietary technology, the Genius Portal, that supports clients, projects, analytics, intelligence and AI initiatives.

It is not simply a SaaS product. It builds on the software a client already relies on.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four connected layers, in order: 01 Data Engineering, 02 Analytics, 03 Business Intelligence, 04 Artificial Intelligence, leading to better business decisions. Each builds on the previous one; they are not four separate products.
- Data Engineering creates the foundation. Analytics turns organized data into understanding. BI makes understanding visible and accessible. AI adds context, reasoning, Second Brain capabilities and AI Agents.
- Homepage only (two versions for comparison: `/` is Version 1, `/v2` is Version 2). No backend, auth, dashboard, database or login.
