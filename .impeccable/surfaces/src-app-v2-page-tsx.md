# Homepage Version 2 surface brief

Scope: /v2 (src/app/v2/page.tsx), a separate comparison version; Version 1 at / stays intact. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: executives. Action: "Talk to us". Proof: none; every demo figure is labeled illustrative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Genius Lab turns business complexity into one living network of intelligence. The page is a network coming alive: scattered data points connect into an irregular mesh that converges on an intelligence core and a decision; the same mesh fills four stacked layers; at the top it becomes a Second Brain made of thousands of connections that agents reason through. Refuses: flat single-colour grounds, regular hex tiles as decoration, glow-for-its-own-sake, the cartographic map of version 1.

OWN-WORLD: Brand navy #101440 deepening to near-black, lit from within: soft radial light blooms, slow undulating line fields and fine point meshes give every dark ground depth. White points and hairline edges at graded opacities are the network; signal blue marks live paths, pulses and the active layer. Hexagons survive only as the four layer plates and the decision crown, rendered as lit, translucent plates with meshed surfaces. Square controls with the wordmark's 45-degree chamfer. Archivo on its width axis; Geist Mono only for system names and readings. White ground for the clarity half (one switch).

STORY: Watch data connect into intelligence; feel the cost of complexity; see the four layers build on each other; watch an agent reason through the Second Brain; learn it is expertise plus the Genius Portal; talk to us.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy with light. Left: pinned headline, pinned subcopy, "Talk to us" plus "See how it works". Across the field: loose labeled data points on the text side, an irregular mesh in the middle, and points drawn into a luminous intelligence core on the right, pulses travelling inward along edges and one line out to a Decision node. The pointer attracts and connects nearby points.

FORM: Living network (user-directed revision of version 2, replacing the hex tiles; no concept roll). Signature interactions: the pointer-reactive hero network, the scroll-built four-layer stack, the point-cloud Second Brain. Motion grammar: connection and convergence, ease-out-expo, pulses only on live paths.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
