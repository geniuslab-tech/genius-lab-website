# Homepage surface brief

Scope: marketing homepage (src/app/page.tsx). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: data and technology leaders evaluating a partner (inferred). Action: "Talk to us". Proof: none exists; all figures are illustrative and labeled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Business data is terrain and Genius Lab surveys it. The page is a survey atlas: raw points (data) are triangulated into a network (connection) and resolved into contour lines whose peak is the read (intelligence). It refuses the category default: navy ground, glowing node web, three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool chalk-white survey paper, cool ink black, contour lines in ink at low alpha, one ultramarine survey ink reserved for the index contour, the active reading and the primary action. One drenched ultramarine plate section. All corners square. Archivo on its width axis (expanded display, normal body) with Geist Mono only for coordinates, elevations and measurements. Hairline graticule ticks frame each plate.

STORY: See data become intelligence in one field; understand the four-layer platform as a terrain cross-section; watch one dataset become a forecast; scan the four disciplines; play with a live atlas; follow the method profile; talk to us.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed live contour canvas. Left 45%: sparse jittering survey points under a two-line expanded headline, subtext, "Talk to us" (ink, square) plus secondary link. Middle: points join into a Delaunay mesh. Right: mesh resolves into contours climbing to a summit marked with an ultramarine index contour and a mono elevation reading. Pointer raises a spring-damped hill. Map annotations name the three zones.

FORM: Topographic survey atlas, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 0cc34c89. Raises: letters as matter (alphabet storm) - CTA display type set at architectural scale cropped by the frame (street poster); rows rerank in place and changed readings hold the accent (gate board); every readout moves with mass and damping, never instant values (VU bridge); the field answers the pointer as a continuous material (ink basin).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
